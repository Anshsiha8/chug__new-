## Brand & Style

The brand personality is punchy, audacious, and unapologetically expressive. It transforms the mundane, clinical routine of drinking water into a high-octane graphic novel experience. Merging Roy Lichtenstein pop art iconography, vintage silver-age comic print production, and high-energy contemporary streetwear, the system energizes the user through tactile visual punch and celebratory visual reward.

The audience consists of visual creatives, streetwear fans, lifestyle maximizers, and gamers who reject sterile, clinical medical trackers. The target emotional response is a burst of dopamine with every sip logged: empowered, playful, collected, and proud. 

The aesthetic is built on Neo-Pop Comic Brutalism. Visuals leverage structural ink lines, raw non-diffused offset drop shadows, rhythmic Benday/halftone screen patterns, dynamic speech balloons, and explosive starbursts for state shifts and gamification triggers.

## Colors

The palette reproduces classic four-color process printing (CMYK) pushed through an electric neon filter, calibrated for an immersive dark mode experience. 

- **Primary Canvas & Surfaces (`#171717`):** Deep ink-black newsprint base. Replaces stark light backgrounds, grounding saturated primary inks and maximizing the visual punch of neon highlights in dark environments.
- **Primary Ink (`#2455FF`):** Electric Cobalt Blue. Represents full hydration, fluid dynamic action, and primary UI interactive states.
- **Secondary Ink (`#FFE14D`):** Classic Comic Yellow. Used for milestones, streaks, gold-tier badges, and high-energy alerts.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#42E8F5`):** Hyper Cyan. Used for quick-hydration logs, secondary fluid counters, and contrasting splash highlights.
- **Punch Accents (`#FF4FA3` Pop Pink & `#FF553D` Vivid Vermilion):** High-priority callouts, dehydrated urgency states, record streaks, and sticker badge trims.
- **Structural Neutral (`#F3F0EF` Bright Inverse Ink):** High-contrast keylines, typography, comic borders, and hard offset drop shadows optimized for dark canvas readability.

All interactive elements maintain high-contrast separation, meeting WCAG AAA compliance against the dark background. Halftone dot overlays utilize translucent cyan (`rgba(66, 232, 245, 0.15)`) and bright ink patterns.

## Typography

The type system creates extreme typographic friction: commanding, condensed display type juxtaposed with sharp, technical geometric sans-serif body copy.

- **Display & Headlines (Anton):** Selected for its punchy vertical proportion, high ink density, and vintage pulp comic aesthetic. Always rendered in uppercase with tight letterforms. Used for major numeric counters (e.g., "2,450 ML"), level-ups, badge titles, and screen headers.
- **Body, UI, & Numbers (Space Grotesk):** Engineered geometry with quirky ink traps that complement the vintage print motif while preserving razor-sharp readability on high-density mobile displays.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Rendered with elevated weights (700) and expanded letter-spacing to emulate authentic pulp-serial caption blocks and editorial diagrams.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors comic book paneling: structured, content-driven frame grids separated by deliberate "gutters."

- **Layout Structure:** On mobile devices, views operate as a 4-column fluid vertical stack wrapped in 16px (`1rem`) outer margins. Tablet devices expand to 8 columns with 24px margins, while desktop screens max out at 12 columns with a locked canvas container of 1080px to preserve tight, poster-like graphic tension.
- **Panel Grid:** Cards and containers snap together using thick ink gutters. UI sections mimic graphic strips, allowing rapid side-swiping between daily hydration timelines and weekly logs.
- **Rhythm:** Spacing follows a 4px/8px modular scale. Dense layout groupings (`space-xs` and `space-sm`) are reserved for metadata badges and pill metrics; larger intervals (`space-lg` and `space-xl`) isolate the primary fluid vessel visualization from secondary interactive trays.

## Elevation & Depth

This system rejects soft, blurred, atmospheric lighting models in favor of hard-edged print production techniques and physical pop-art layering:

- **Hard Offset Shadows:** Surfaces do not fade into blurred darkness. All elevated components project a razor-sharp, zero-blur drop shadow tailored for dark mode separation: `box-shadow: 4px 4px 0px #F3F0EF`. On click or press states, components translate 2px down and right (`transform: translate(2px, 2px)`), reducing the shadow to `2px 2px 0px #F3F0EF` to simulate mechanical physical actuation.
- **Structural Outlines:** Every card, button, floating bar, and bubble is bound by a solid `3px solid #F3F0EF` keyline stroke. Complex hero vessels use `4px solid #F3F0EF`.
- **Halftone Texture Layers:** Secondary elevation and interior volume are indicated via repeating SVG/CSS radial dot patterns (Benday dots) positioned inside fluid chambers and card headers, evoking 1960s lithographic newsprint screen angles.
- **Overlapping Z-Panels:** Layering is achieved by tilting and offset-stacking panels at deliberate `-1deg` to `+2.5deg` angles, mimicking stickers and physical comic book cutouts.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary balances bold blocky geometries with expressive comic visual metaphors:

- **Default Radii:** Interactive containers and cards use a roundedness level of `2` (`0.5rem` / `8px` corner radius) wrapped in thick ink strokes. This softens mechanical brutalism while retaining structural comic-strip containment.
- **Action Pills:** Primary logging pills, quick-add counters, and badge chips utilize full pill contours (`9999px`) to invite thumb interaction.
- **Graphic Callouts:** Feedback banners and notification toasts use asymmetrical speech balloons with triangular indicator tails pointing directly toward the dynamic hydration tank or target tracker.
- **Milestone Starbursts:** Streak celebrations and completion summaries employ 8-point and 12-point multi-angled polygon masks (`clip-path: polygon(...)`) with contrasting yellow and pop-pink fill layers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Quick Log):** Heavy electric cobalt blue (`#2455FF`) background, `3px solid #F3F0EF` border, `4px 4px 0px #F3F0EF` offset shadow. Label rendered in Anton, uppercase, light ink (`#171717`). Active state collapses offset to `2px 2px 0px` with a `translate(2px, 2px)`.
- **Secondary Action (Adjust/Custom):** Dark surface background (`#171717`) with electric yellow (`#FFE14D`) text highlight, identical 3px ink stroke and light offset shadow.
- **Icon / FAB Buttons:** Perfect circles (`56px x 56px`), thick border, containing clean vector glyphs bounded in solid ink.

### Interactive Fluid Tank (Hero Component)
- Custom volumetric cylinder or beaker frame bounded by a `4px solid #F3F0EF` stroke.
- Liquid fill represented by flat cyan (`#42E8F5`) transitioning into electric cobalt (`#2455FF`) via a hard stepped threshold line rather than a smooth gradient.
- Halftone pattern floats over the liquid surface. Floating bubble badges inside the tank indicate logged milliliter bursts.

### Cards & Panels
- Background: `#171717` or deep dark surface tint.
- Outer border: `3px solid #F3F0EF`.
- Shadow: `4px 4px 0px #F3F0EF`.
- Header tags: Miniature rectangular sticker badges affixed to top-left borders with 0-degree or slight negative tilts (`transform: rotate(-1.5deg)`).

### Input Fields
- Heavy rectangular boxes framed in `3px solid #F3F0EF`.
- Surface: Flat dark tint or `#171717`.
- Focus State: Hard shadow changes color to electric cobalt (`box-shadow: 4px 4px 0px #2455FF`), no fuzzy focus rings. Placeholder text displayed in muted inverse ink at 50% opacity.

### Chips & Quick-Add Pills
- Small rounded interactive buttons for volume increments (`+250ml`, `+500ml`, `+750ml`).
- Default: `#171717` surface, `2px solid #F3F0EF`, `2px 2px 0px #F3F0EF`.
- Selected/Tapped: `#FFE14D` fill, black bold Space Grotesk type, immediate tactile depression.

### Checkboxes & Toggle Controls
- Custom square box (`24px x 24px`) with `3px solid #F3F0EF` stroke.
- Unchecked: `#171717` background.
- Checked: Electric cobalt fill with an oversized, comic-style check mark (`✓`) or lightning bolt glyph.
- Switch toggles use rectangular slider tracks with circular solid thumbs that slide with zero-inertia snap.

### Speech Bubble Toasts & Banners
- Contextual reminders ("TIME TO GULP!", "HALFWAY THERE, HERO!") encased in authentic comic speech dialogue bubbles.
- Features a bottom or side vector pointer/tail anchored directly to the hydration avatar or daily progress ring.
